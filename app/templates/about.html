About {{ app_name }}

At {{ app_name }}, we are passionate about producing the freshest, highest-quality dairy products. Our farm is located in Kapcholyo, Kaplong and its environs, where our cows are kept in zero-grazing units, fed nutritious fodder, and milked hygienically twice daily — morning and evening.

We believe in farm-direct delivery - no middlemen, no delays. From our cows to your doorstep, every drop of milk is fresh, nutritious, and delicious.

Our Values

  • Fresh milk daily from happy, healthy cows
  • Strict hygiene and quality standards
  • Direct farm-to-door delivery
  • Sustainable, ethical farming practices
