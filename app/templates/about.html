Our Story

How Chegoland Started

Chegoland began with a simple belief: every family deserves milk that is fresh, clean and honestly produced. Located in Kapcholyo, Kaplong and its environs, our zero-grazing dairy unit keeps happy, healthy cows fed on nutritious fodder and milked hygienically twice daily — morning and evening.

We cut out the middlemen so milk goes straight from our cows to your doorstep. No delays, no compromises — just fresh, nutritious, locally produced dairy, every single day.

Our Process

From Cow to You

Four simple steps that keep every drop fresh.

1

Milking

Our cows are milked hygienically twice daily — morning and evening — in clean zero-grazing stalls.

2

Testing

Every batch is tested for purity, fat content and hygiene before it is approved for packing.

3

Packaging

Milk is packed in clean, sealed containers under strict cold-chain conditions.

4

Delivery

Your order is delivered fresh to your doorstep in Kapcholyo, Kaplong and surrounding towns.
